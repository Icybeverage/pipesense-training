    Full shipped stack

    • MediaPipe hand telemetry (21 landmarks per hand) drives articulated virtual glove motion and thumb-index pinch grabs.
    • Deterministic browser simulation scores sequence, alignment, tightening, leaks, retained water, and sewer-gas seal.
    • W&B Weave traces the attempt loop while W&B Inference rewrites coaching language and intervention suggestions.
    • ElevenLabs Live powers Maya as an opt-in coach, with captions/text fallback when voice is off or unavailable.

    What W&B actually does

    • pipesense/attempt-loop traces nested observer, coach, evaluator and inference operations.
    • W&B Inference can rewrite coaching language; it cannot change deterministic correctness or score.
    • Retry policy stays bounded: visual target, kinesthetic snap assist, or step-by-step reset.
    • Evaluation compares deterministic score against the previous attempt and keeps or replaces strategy.

    Deterministic scoring: The browser simulation computes geometry, sequencing, score, and pass/fail.

    W&B inference: Optional provider output rewrites coaching text and strategy suggestion, then Weave traces the loop state.
